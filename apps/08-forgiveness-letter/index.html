THE WELL · NO. 8

THE FORGIVENESS LETTER

Write the letter
you’ll never send.

To the parent, the ex, the friend — or to yourself.
Three honest prompts. Then release it, seal it, or burn it.
No one ever reads it but you and God.

Nothing you write leaves this page.
No account. No history. No one will ever know you were here.

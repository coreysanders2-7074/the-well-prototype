THE UNSENT PRAYER

Say the thing
you’d never say out loud.

Angry? Doubting? Embarrassed?
It belongs here — and it stays here.

Nothing you write leaves this page.
No account. No history. It’s gone the moment you let it go.

THE WELL HEARD IT

The Well heard it.
No flinching. No fixing. Just heard.

Trust in him at all times, you people; pour out your hearts to him, for God is our refuge.

PSALM 62:8 · NIV

If you’re thinking about harming yourself, stop and call or text 988 right now. Someone trained and kind is there 24/7. You matter more than this moment.

What should the Well do with your words?

SEALED

✝

Sealed. Your words rest with the Well for 7 days —
and they were never attached to you.
Nothing was stored. Nothing was sent.
When the 7 days pass, there will be nothing to delete —
because there was never anything to keep.

LET IT GO

It’s gone.
You’re still held.

The Well is still here
whenever you need it.

No record. No residue. Just a little more
room to breathe than when you arrived.
